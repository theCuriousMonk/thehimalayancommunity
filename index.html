✨Living mountain index

Stillness, but never static.

The Himalayan Community is a showcase layer for the people and places that make the valley breathe: what to visit, where to stay, where to sit, where to ride, and what is quietly happening under the ridge.

Restaurants, community tables, cultural nights, village celebrations, and people outside.
